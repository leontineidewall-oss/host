05 · FAQ

Questions

Who gets the drops?

The giant's top 500 holders at the snapshot. Pools, exchanges and burn addresses are skipped.

Why pay another coin's holders?

Because they notice. Every drop lands in hundreds of wallets that already believe in the giant, with $HOST's name on it.

How do I know it happened?

Each round is sent from the drop wallet with the memo host:drop:<round>. This page reads them straight from the chain.
